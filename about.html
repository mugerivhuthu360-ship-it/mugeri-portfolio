About

A little about me

I'm Mugeri Vhuthuhawe — a third-year Electrical Engineering student and a working keys player, based in South Africa. I build systems by day and perform by night.

I'm completing a Diploma in Electrical Engineering at Walter Sisulu University. In parallel I serve as Main AUX Keys Player for LMAE Sounds and as keys player at CRC East London — gospel and live band work, session recording, and a self-built performance rig.

My credits include live and session work alongside Ziyanda Tshangana and Andile Tsako. On the technology side I build and run my own rigs in Ableton Live 12 with virtual instruments and MIDI routing — the same discipline I apply to circuit design.

Goal: keep developing as an engineering professional while staying active as a performer and music technologist, and let the two sides inform each other.

Currently: third-year Electrical Engineering studies at WSU, resident keys for LMAE Sounds and CRC East London, and building out an Ableton-based live rig.
