Frequently asked

What kind of engineering work are you open to?

Internships, graduate placements, part-time technical roles, lab assistance and project-based work while I complete my Diploma. I'm honest about what I can do and quick to learn what I can't yet.

What does a live keys booking involve?

I handle the parts, the sounds and the setup — from a single keyboard to a full Ableton-based rig with layered sounds and expression control. I prepare in advance and adapt on the night.

Can you record keyboard parts for a track?

Yes — session keyboard recording, part development and arrangement. I prepare sounds and parts before the session and record clean, usable tracks. Credit and permission agreed up front.
