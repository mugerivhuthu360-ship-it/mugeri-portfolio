Music technology

01 — Ableton Live AUX Keys Rig

A performance-ready keyboard setup built around Ableton Live 12. The rig brings together MIDI controllers, virtual instruments, an audio interface and expression control so that sounds can be organised, layered and recalled reliably in a live setting.

What I focus on

  • Signal flow from controller to software to output
  • MIDI routing and instrument management
  • Expression pedal mapping
  • Sound selection and layering across keys and pads
  • Organising sessions so changes are fast during a service or engagement

Tools: Ableton Live 12, Omnisphere, Keyscape, Zenology Pro, MK Sensations Extreme / PadLab, Novation Launchkey 61 MK4, Focusrite Scarlett Solo.

03 — Session Keyboard Work

Recording keyboard parts and adapting arrangements for recorded and live music. With session work, being clear about exactly what I contributed matters as much as the playing itself.

How I approach it

  • Prepare parts and sounds before the session
  • Record clean, usable keyboard tracks
  • Adapt voicings and layers to the music
  • Agree credit and permission before sharing material

Any recordings shown here are shared only with the appropriate permission.
